48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint

Sample Service Company · New-inquiry qualification and follow-up

Important: This fictional example demonstrates the format and decision quality of the paid Blueprint. It is not a client result, case study, forecast, or guarantee.

1. Executive decision

Recommended pilot

Use AI to prepare a structured lead summary and a channel-appropriate reply draft. A named employee reviews every draft before it is sent.

Do not automate yet

Pricing exceptions, service-area disputes, safety questions, refunds, and unhappy-customer messages stay fully human-owned.

2. Current workflow

Website inquiry→Shared inbox→Manual triage→Reply drafted→Follow-up remembered

Observed friction: incomplete inquiry data, inconsistent response structure, unclear ownership, and no reliable follow-up checkpoint.

3. Proposed supervised workflow

StageAI preparesHuman ownsException
IntakeExtracts requested service, location, urgency, and missing fields.Confirms source and permission.Ignore attachments until reviewed.
QualificationApplies approved fit criteria and flags uncertainty.Accepts or corrects classification.Route borderline cases to owner.
ResponseDrafts a reply from approved facts and tone.Approves before send.No pricing promises or guarantees.

4. Recommended stack

Start with existing tools

Website form, current inbox, spreadsheet or CRM, and one approved language model. Add software only after the pilot exposes a real need.

Access boundary

Read only the designated intake source. Draft-only email permission. No autonomous publishing, payment changes, deletions, or customer commitments.

6. 30-day rollout

Days 1–7

Confirm policy, map fields, collect baseline, approve examples, and test with synthetic records.

Days 8–14

Run draft-only pilot with daily review and a small, bounded volume.

Days 15–21

Review errors, edge cases, timing, staff feedback, and customer-response quality.

Days 22–30

Keep, revise, or stop. Expand only with written approval and a rollback plan.

7. Delivery boundaries

The paid Blueprint includes a workflow map, AI opportunity brief, recommended stack, 30-day implementation roadmap, and one revision. Implementation, integrations, software fees, and guaranteed outcomes are not included. The 48-hour clock starts after payment, completed intake, the 45-minute workflow call, and receipt of required inputs; client-caused delays pause the clock.
