## 15-second pitch

AgentForce HQ helps service businesses figure out where AI is actually worth using. We map one repetitive workflow, show what AI should prepare, where a person must approve, what can go wrong, and give the business a 30-day rollout plan. Jarvis coordinates a specialist AI team, but the customer keeps final control.

## 60-second pitch

Most small businesses do not need more AI subscriptions. They need one workflow fixed. AgentForce HQ starts with a $299 48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint. We audit one process such as lead follow-up, proposal preparation, scheduling, onboarding, reporting, or internal knowledge retrieval. A specialist AI team analyzes the workflow, security and compliance are reviewed where relevant, and Jarvis turns the findings into one decision-ready plan. The customer receives the current-state map, top bottlenecks, supervised future-state workflow, safeguards, capacity scenarios, KPI plan, and a 30-day rollout. If the plan makes sense, implementation is a separate optional scope.

## Objection handling

### “We already use ChatGPT.”

AgentForce is not another chat window. The Blueprint maps a real business process, defines inputs and outputs, sets approval points, handles exceptions, adds safeguards, and defines how success will be measured.

### “I don’t want AI talking directly to customers.”

That is not required. Draft-only workflows are a normal starting point. AI can prepare information or responses while a person reviews every consequential action.

### “How do I know this will save money?”

The Blueprint does not guarantee savings. It models low, base, and high capacity scenarios with visible assumptions so the buyer can decide whether implementation is justified.

### “Why pay $299 before implementation?”

The Blueprint prevents paying to automate the wrong process. It produces a vendor-ready plan that can be implemented by AgentForce, the buyer’s internal team, or another provider.

### “Can you just build it?”

Yes, after the workflow is scoped. Implementation is separately quoted so the buyer knows what should be built, what safeguards are required, and what success looks like before custom work begins.

## What not to promise

Do not promise guaranteed profit, guaranteed savings, guaranteed conversion lifts, full autonomy, legal/compliance approval, cybersecurity certification, or replacement of human judgment.
