# AgentForce HQ — Product Specification

## Positioning

**AgentForce HQ** is a productized, human-supervised AI workforce service. It helps a business identify one repetitive workflow, design a controlled AI-assisted version, define human approval gates and failure controls, and create a measurable rollout plan.

Primary promise:

> Build an AI department. Keep the human decision.

Primary entry offer:

> 48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint — $299 founding offer.

The Blueprint is a decision-ready workflow design engagement, not a promise of profit in 48 hours.

## Human-approval model

AgentForce is supervised. Sensitive or consequential actions remain human-owned. Examples include customer-facing commitments, publishing, payments, pricing changes, deletions, access changes, legal/compliance determinations, safety decisions, and similar high-impact actions.

Default route:

**Specialists → Trust review where relevant → Jarvis → Human approval**

## Offer ladder

### 1. 48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint — $299

Includes:

- 45-minute workflow audit
- Current-state map for one company, one team, and one workflow
- Top-three bottleneck/opportunity ranking
- One supervised future-state workflow
- Triggers, actions, exceptions, and human approval gates
- Up to two tool recommendations
- Low/base/high illustrative capacity scenarios with visible assumptions
- Privacy, security, and failure-control checklist
- 30-day rollout sequence with owners and success measures
- 20-minute handoff
- One revision requested within seven calendar days
- Workflow-intake/SOP template
- Human-approval and safeguard checklist
- 30-day KPI tracker
- Vendor-ready implementation handoff

The 48-hour clock starts after cleared payment, completed intake, the 45-minute workflow call, and receipt of required inputs. Client-caused delays or rescheduling pause the clock.

### 2. AgentForce Workflow Build — Custom scope

Optional implementation of the approved Blueprint. Typical scope may include integrations, workflow automation, retrieval, prompt/system design, testing, monitoring, and human approval gates. Software licenses, third-party usage, migration, and custom engineering are quoted separately.

### 3. Managed AgentOps — Monthly

Post-implementation operations may include:

- KPI reviews
- Quality monitoring
- Prompt/workflow tuning
- Failure and exception review
- Security/compliance checks
- Prioritized improvements
- Monthly operating summary

## Ideal buyer

Prioritize owner-led, high-ticket service firms with roughly 3–25 employees that already use a CRM, assistant, administrator, or repeatable back-office process but still handle intake, estimates, proposals, scheduling, onboarding, reporting, or follow-up manually.

Strong initial verticals:

- Contractors and home services
- Agencies and consultancies
- Commercial cleaning and property services
- Regional managed-service providers
- Recruiters and professional-services teams where the workflow is not highly regulated

## Qualification rule

Sell the Blueprint when all of the following are true:

1. The buyer can name one repetitive workflow.
2. The workflow has a measurable bottleneck, delay, cost, quality issue, or capacity problem.
3. A human owner can review and approve the future-state process.
4. The required information can be provided without sharing unnecessary sensitive records or credentials.
5. The buyer understands the Blueprint is workflow design, not guaranteed savings or revenue.

## Risk reversal

- If the paid audit identifies no suitable workflow, refund the $299 rather than manufacture a recommendation.
- If an agreed deliverable is missing, correct it within two business days after notice or refund the fee.
- Implementation is optional and separately scoped.

## Core sales message

> Most businesses do not need another AI subscription. They need one workflow mapped clearly: what AI prepares, what a person approves, what happens when something goes wrong, and what to measure. AgentForce HQ delivers that plan in 48 hours through a Jarvis-orchestrated specialist team.
