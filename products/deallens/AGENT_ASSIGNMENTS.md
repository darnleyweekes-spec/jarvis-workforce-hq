# DealLens Agent Assignments

Jarvis is the orchestration and final AI review layer. The human owner retains final approval for consequential external actions.

## Context + orchestration
- Context Architect: maintain the deal context packet, assumptions, constraints, evidence requirements, freshness rules, and output schema.
- Agent Orchestrator / Jarvis: route work, enforce dependencies, reconcile conflicts, and run final review.

- PAM / JIM: ICP, user workflow, MVP scope, acceptance criteria, buyer feedback loop.
- FRED: forward-deployed discovery, client implementation design, integration, onboarding, and rollout.

## Trust + safety

## Growth
- Scout: find high-fit search funds, independent sponsors, boutique M&A advisors, family offices, and small PE teams.
- Maker: landing page, sample deliverable, demo narrative, sales collateral.
- Closer: founding-customer offer, discovery script, objection handling, proposal structure.
- Echo: follow-up cadence and feedback collection.
- Judge / Evaluator: score deliverables for factuality, citations, usefulness, and decision quality.

## Required cross-check before client delivery
1. Deterministic calculations reproduced independently.
2. Every material AI claim has evidence or is labeled hypothesis.
3. Source conflicts are surfaced, not silently resolved.
4. Prompt-injection and malicious-document checks run.
5. Confidential information stays inside the deal workspace.
6. Human reviewer signs off before delivery.
