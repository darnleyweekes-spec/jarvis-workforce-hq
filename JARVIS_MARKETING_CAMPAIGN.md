# AgentForce HQ — Jarvis Marketing Campaign

## Campaign objective

Turn live traffic and outbound attention into conversations around one specific business workflow, then convert qualified prospects through this funnel:

**Free 3-point workflow teardown → $299 48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint → AgentForce Workflow Build → Managed AgentOps**

The campaign must sell the business outcome and workflow clarity first. The 23-agent roster is supporting proof, not the opening message.

### Low-friction CTA

**Send one workflow for a free 3-point teardown.**

### Paid CTA

**48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint — $299 founding offer.**

### Upgrade path

If the Blueprint identifies a worthwhile project:
1. AgentForce Workflow Build

## Priority campaign audiences

### Tier 1 — fastest fit

- Marketing agencies
- Consultants
- HR service firms
- Real estate teams
- Commercial property firms
- Home-service and contracting companies
- Small regional MSPs

Best workflows:
- Lead intake and routing
- Proposal/SOW preparation
- Estimate follow-up
- Client onboarding
- Recurring reporting
- Internal knowledge retrieval
- Project handoffs
- Administrative follow-up

### Tier 2 — controlled professional workflows

- Accounting firms
- Law firms
- Engineering firms
- Logistics companies

Position around administrative or operational preparation only. Human professionals retain judgment and approval.

### Tier 3 — enterprise / regulated

- Banks
- Healthcare systems
- Insurance and health-plan organizations
- Large financial firms

Do not use the standard $299 SMB cold pitch as the opening offer. Lead with governance, controlled pilots, back-office preparation, auditability, and human authorization.

### Day 2 — Workflow example

Theme: slow lead follow-up.

Hook:

> A lead does not need a fully autonomous AI sales rep. It may only need a system that summarizes the inquiry, flags missing information, drafts the next response, and routes it to a person quickly.

CTA: free teardown.

### Day 3 — Before / after process

Before:
- Inquiry arrives
- Someone notices it
- Information is incomplete
- Reply is rebuilt manually
- Follow-up depends on memory

After supervised design:
- Inquiry is structured
- Missing information is flagged
- Draft is prepared
- Human approves
- Follow-up checkpoint is created

CTA: request teardown or view sample Blueprint.

### Day 4 — Objection post

Theme: “We already use ChatGPT.”

Message:

> ChatGPT is a tool. A workflow defines what triggers the work, what information is allowed, what AI prepares, where a person approves, what happens when something goes wrong, and how success is measured.

### Day 5 — ROI without hype

Theme: capacity, not guaranteed profit.

Message:

> Before automating anything, estimate how many repetitive hours the workflow consumes, what those hours cost, and how much work could realistically be redirected. If the economics do not justify implementation, stop there.

Link to the live capacity calculator.

### Day 6 — Trust post

Theme: human-controlled AI.

Message:

> The goal is not “remove the human.” The goal is “remove repetitive preparation while keeping the human where judgment matters.”

Show route:

**Specialists → Trust review → Jarvis → Human approval**

### Day 7 — Founding offer

Message:

> If your team has one repetitive workflow that is clearly costing time, start with the free teardown. If it looks worth pursuing, the 48-Hour AI Workflow Blueprint is $299 and produces the current-state map, future-state supervised workflow, safeguards, capacity scenarios, KPI plan, and 30-day rollout.
