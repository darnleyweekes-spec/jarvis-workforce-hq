Management diligence questions

1. The largest customer represents approximately 42% of supplied revenue. What is the renewal date, termination language, pricing history and executive relationship ownership for this account?
2. The top five customers represent approximately 90% of supplied revenue. What downside case has management modeled for loss or repricing of one or more of these accounts?
3. Reconcile revenue growth by customer, service/product line, price and volume. Identify any one-time items or acquisition-related changes.
4. What working-capital seasonality or transaction-related cash requirements are not visible in the supplied current-ratio calculation?
