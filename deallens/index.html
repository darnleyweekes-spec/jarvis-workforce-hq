The workflow

From data room to decision-ready questions.

The first pass should organize evidence and expose uncertainty — not manufacture conviction. DealLens separates reproducible calculations from AI-assisted evidence review.

01 / INGEST

Bring the deal materials

CIMs, PDFs, spreadsheets, customer revenue exports and supporting files enter one review workflow.

02 / VERIFY

Calculate what should be calculated

Revenue growth, margins, net debt, liquidity and customer concentration are calculated deterministically from supplied structured data.

03 / REVIEW

Surface evidence, not guesses

Risk excerpts receive source IDs. Management questions are generated from actual evidence and financial conditions, then a human reviewer approves the packet.

Built for a smaller deal team

DealLens is not another data-room system. It is designed for teams where principals still touch the work and every hour spent on a weak opportunity has an opportunity cost.

Best fit: lower-middle-market opportunities where you already have initial deal materials and need a disciplined first pass before expanding third-party diligence.
Trust architecture

Human-supervised by design.

EVIDENCE

Source traceability

Material flags are tied to source excerpts and evidence IDs so reviewers can inspect what drove the question.

FINANCE

No invented arithmetic

Core financial checks use deterministic functions rather than relying on an LLM to calculate deal metrics.

SECURITY

Documents are untrusted

Uploaded document instructions never override DealLens rules. Prompt-like content is treated as untrusted and surfaced for review.

DealLens accelerates first-pass review. It does not replace legal, tax, accounting, quality-of-earnings, cybersecurity or other specialist diligence, and it does not make autonomous investment decisions.
