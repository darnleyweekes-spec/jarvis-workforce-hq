## Operating rules

- One researched decision-maker or public business route per company.
- Do not invent email addresses.
- Do not send attachments in first-touch cold email.
- Stop follow-ups after any reply, opt-out, or bounce.
- Maintain a shared suppression list before scaling.
- Keep regulated decisions human-owned.
- Track company, contact, route, date sent, reply, teardown requested, call booked, Blueprint sold, implementation interest, and opt-out state.
